# Immediate first review and 30-minute repeats

- A newly weak question is due immediately. A confident correct answer advances the graduation count; the next qualifying attempt is available after 30 minutes. Two qualifying correct answers graduate it.
- Wrong or uncertain answers to an already weak question reset the count and schedule it 30 minutes later. Early confident correct practice does not advance the count or postpone the deadline.
- Existing 24-hour schedules migrate once to the shorter policy; totals, correctness and earned review passes are retained. Historical unanswered-statistics repair retains its original timeline semantics before schedule migration.
- Every new review session shuffles IDs. Identical consecutive order for the same list of two or more questions is prevented. Persisted IDs/answers keep their mapping on reload and resume. Single-question sessions naturally cannot change order.
- The review screen refreshes due counts as time passes and on returning to the page. It restores focused review controls when refreshing and retains the expanded policy explanation.
- The separate answer-screen confirmation dialog remains unscored practice and says so explicitly.

Validation: full npm test passed, including immediate first eligibility, 30-minute-minus-1ms versus exact-boundary qualification, wrong/uncertain reset, old-schedule migration and idempotence, shuffled review resume, all ten randomized mocks, partial-exam scoring and historical repair, 500 question texts and 130 image bindings.

Physical iPhone/Safari was not tested; exact time progression was tested with an injected clock.
